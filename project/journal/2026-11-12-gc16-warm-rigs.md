# GC16: the walk into Sunscar met Driftwood's freed creature coat

> Written by: Claude (opus-gc16), 2026-11-12

GC15c's page walk stopped 1 m inside Sunscar: the crossing's preparation went `blocked` on
`Compressed texture shardfile:a3fca362… lost its allocation after mip retirement`. Only Driftwood's book names that
texture, so the next cell's preparation was touching the world the player had just left.

A diagnostic build with source maps put the throw in `uploadCompressedTexture`'s `resident()` check, from the precompile's
texture phase in `Game.prepareEnteredPrograms`. Walking the live scene for the texture's users found one hidden group
straight under the page scene: `creature-warm`, holding Driftwood's `driftwood-creature-captain` rig.

That group is DL24's parked warm rigs (`AnimalHerdView.stageWarmRigs`): one hidden rig per creature model, kept on the
page scene "for the level's life" so the first frame's warm draws build their programs. On the grid the boot level is
only Driftwood's cell. Leaving it freed the captain's coat, but the parked rig stayed in the scene, and the entry
preparation walks hidden roots on purpose (a parked destination is hidden), so it tried to make the freed, already
mip-retired texture resident.

The fix: each parked rig listens for `dispose` on every material and texture it draws with (maps, shader-patch
samplers, uniforms) and leaves the scene the moment one is freed; re-staging releases the old listeners.
`src/client/engine/entities/warm-rig-lease.test.ts` covers both.

A first guess, taking a retiring region's root out of the scene before its stepped disposal (`retireRegion`), did not
change the walk and was dropped: the rig was never under the region's root.

Review (one-shot `claude -p`), no MUSTs. Taken: render-target textures (PMREM, shadow maps) are the page's and are
replaced while the level lives, so they no longer take a rig out (that would stop later re-warming); arrays of uniform
textures count. Left: the lease hangs off `pageScope`, not the level's owner, so a rig whose resources are never freed
stays parked as it did before this change.
