# MMO MP2.5 review follow-ups (opus-mmo5)

- MP2.5e (review S3): the door's /account/delete and /account/export took any live join ticket.
  A join ticket lasts an hour and rides every hello. They now take only the api-server's
  five-minute confirm:<player>.<expires> ticket, minted for a live session on the tap
  (api-server 1351bda, POST /api/players/me/confirm). admit() refuses a confirm as a hello;
  confirmed() refuses a join ticket and any confirm reaching past ten minutes. Picked over
  "recent sign-in" because the API's own delete already trusts the session: a fresh mail
  per delete adds friction and no safety.
- MP2.5f/g (review S2, S5): one rule for every player that goes, an account delete or a guest
  expiry: saves/account.ts anonymise() moves receipts to a fresh deleted:<uuid> per player and
  re-keys chat reports to it (reporter or speaker; a reported Name reads "Deleted"), keeping the
  typed and shown text as moderation evidence. Guest expiry used to delete receipts; it now
  keeps them anonymously, so the ledger adds up either way.
- MP2.2a staging proof: scripts/staging-sign-in-proof.mjs mails signin@proof.wildshard.io from
  the staging card, reads the link from api-server's new wildshard-proof-inbox (Email Routing on
  proof.wildshard.io to KV), redeems it in Chromium and WebKit, then deletes the proof account.
