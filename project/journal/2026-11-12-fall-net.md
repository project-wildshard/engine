# 2026-11-12 fall net (ENG-18, opus-eng18)

- Why falls went on for ever: the net lived in the hosts, not the sim. The page has
  `installBounds` (authored bounds only, or the grid's -60), a net room's seats have
  cellWorld's `FallFloor` (and a lone cell has edge walls). The trusted resident on its
  own (headless tapes, the bake worker, shard CI journeys) had none, so Dunes' south
  basin (shard-dunes `9b67f3f`) and Sky Reach's drum step (shard-sky-reach `a8369a9`)
  fell to y -6 000 and on. The room already caught both.
- Fix: `residentFallNet` (fallFloor.ts) runs after each resident step when it is not a
  room: under `shardFallFloor` (the manifest floor, never under -CHUNK_HALF) a player goes
  back to their last firm ground, else the spawn. The page also gets a floor (-CHUNK_HALF)
  for a level with no authored bounds outside the grid (`playBounds`).
- `test/fall-net.test.ts` walks both repros and drops a player under the ground, on the
  bare resident and in a DedicatedRoom; red on the resident before the fix.
- The net's last firm spot is not in a snapshot: a restored resident goes back to the
  spawn until it has stood somewhere again.
- Follow-up: the resident's net waits 0.25 s off every collider under the floor
  (`RESIDENT_FALL_GRACE`). Sky Reach's edge sky docks sit at y 0 under its floor 12, and
  `physics-shards-headless` walks them on a bare resident: an instant net took the walker
  on its first airborne tick. A room's `FallFloor` keeps grace 0, as before; its seats on
  those docks may be reset the same way (left for a follow-up row).
- Test fix: a world's colliders answer `floorBelow` only after its first step, so the test
  steps twice before standing the player on the drum step (y 44).
