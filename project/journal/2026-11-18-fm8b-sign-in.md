# Sign-in that ends or fails says so

> Written by: Claude
>
> **State:** built 2026-11-18, opus-fm2, meta MMO-FAILURE-MODES-PLAN FM7b3c and FM8b (modes 27 to 29).

Before this, a session the API ended was silent. The 20-minute ticket
refresh got a 401, signed the page out, and the next hello joined as the
device's guest: the player's account was quietly not the one playing.
A run-out ticket at the door did the same.

The door (FM7b3c): a ticket its key signed that has run out by the
door's clock is refused with FM7b1's `auth-expired` close fact. A ticket
no key here signed claims nothing and still plays as its device. A fresh
ticket for the same device plays as the account.

The page (FM8b):

- 27 `auth-expired`: a card over play, Sign in (the existing card; a
  page signed in when it closes rejoins as the account), Play as a guest
  (rejoins as the device), Title. It is opened by the API's own 401 (the
  refresh, or the ticket ask each drop now makes) or by the door's close,
  after which a session that still holds just rejoins with a fresh
  ticket. No page clock decides it.
- 28 `auth-failed`: a mailed link the API refused (401) opens the
  sign-in card on Sign-in didn't work, with Email me a new link.
- 29 `api-5xx`: the API's 500, 502, 503 or 504 (not its own "mail is
  off" 503) says Accounts are down, the status under Details; Try now is the player's tap,
  Play as a guest closes it. A network failure claims nothing of the API.

The browser proof drives an owned stand-in for api-server's player
routes (`scripts/failmode-api-fixture.mjs`) on the proxy's `api` route,
with the rig door checking that run's ticket key. Seats are read from
the rig's own saves (pglite:).
