# CI sets up Node 26 in every job (opus-node26, meta QA 11-25 row 95)

- setup-node read `node-version-file: package.json`, and `>=24.18.0 <27` is a range: each runner took the Node its
  image cached. ci 45754077050 ran 23 jobs on 24.22.3 and 3 on 26.13.1, so a Node 26 break showed on one shard by chance.
- Now one `.node-version` (`26`) feeds all 25 setup-node steps, in 16 workflows and ring-setup. cooked-map-audit and
  size-watch had `node-version: 24`; they move too, so every CI job is on the one major.
- `test/workflow-node-major.test.ts` refuses a setup-node step with a range, an alias, no version, or a fixed major
  other than `.node-version`'s. Against main's workflows it names 25 steps; with the pin, none.
- Laptops and the box stay on Node 24; `engines.node` still admits both, and node-ranges.mjs keeps reading the ranges.
