# An Open Plot's music after a real cross-in: absent (AU14)

> Written by: Claude (opus-au14b), 2026-11-13

AU14's proof heard every deck shard's music and sound effects, but not an Open Plot's. A plot has no deck card, and its
deep link enters by `&skipintro`, the bench path that never runs ENTER WORLD, so it said nothing about the plot.

`scripts/audio-download-proof.mjs` now has a `cross` route, preview only (staging has no `WORLD_CROSSING`, and the
local build's world door refuses loopback). It picks the plot's deck neighbour from Shard select, enters, and walks
opus-sa34b's road from Pine Hollow into the ring-2 plot at (0, 2), Saltwind Lighthouse, through the in-page server. The
window is the first 10 s after the feet stand in the plot's cell, watched to 30 s. It also checks that the music's
score becomes the plot's own (`audio.music.id`), and counts the audio the page read on the crossing.

What it found, on a preview build of `cd576457`, headless WebKit 402×874, warm and slow:

- Sound effects play: footsteps on the sfx bus 50 to 70 ms after the crossing.
- Music doesn't play. The music bus stays under −60 dBFS for all 30 s, and so does the ambience bus. Pine's score
  goes when the feet leave Pine for the road (score `null`, 9 s before the plot), and the plot's score never comes.
- The grid's live session makes the plot `current` and gameplay-ready, but the hybrid runtime session never enters it:
  its hook timings list only Pine Hollow's stages. A hooked `music.setScore` was never called after Pine's. The plot's
  `audio.scene` feature (its client plan) installs the score, beds and loops only on entry, so none of them run.
- The plot does declare music: `audio.music` id `saltwind-lighthouse`, slot `score`, and two takes in its mounts.
- LOAD made 0 audio requests, and the crossing read no audio at all. Pine's grid DOWNLOAD holds none of the plot's
  audio, so a plot that did start its score would read its takes during play.
