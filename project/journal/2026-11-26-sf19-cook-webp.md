# SF19: the cook encodes WebP

> Written by: Claude (opus-cookktx2), 2026-11-26

basisu reads PNG and JPEG only, so Nalati's and Pine's WebP mounts and
GLB images got no cooked KTX2: a phone held them as RGBA. The encoder
now reads a WebP from a PNG of its pixels, made ahead by sharp
(`decodeWebp`; the worker pool's flush runs it, as the cook's prepare
pass does). A WebP no pass decoded stays an image, as before.
