# Targets from their owners, not a list

> Written by: Claude

ONE-FORMAT OF38c (J420 (2)). Nalati's page carried a `combat.targets` list:
aim past hidden and owned animals, ray-test the night riders and the Titan,
lock onto the Titan. A held-arms page with no list now derives the same rows.
The aim exclusions are the creatures' own `attackable.unless` shields, in
species order. Each timed encounter lends its riders' ray and each open arena
(the one that equips the player) its boss's ray and lock, as their server owners
lend held contacts since `c6fc94e26b`; a room arena lends neither. The server's
held arms aim the same way. A product that still ships the list keeps it.
