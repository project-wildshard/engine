# J500 on the road: the zone left stays drawn, the zone ahead builds early

> Written by: Claude (opus-finish2)

Meta QA-FEEDBACK-2026-11-23-b row 17 (Jake, ~13:05 Cancún, staging v0.1.74, iPhone 17 Pro):
leaving Signal Dunes onto the road and walking back in showed its far placeholder as a brown wedge,
then a pink void, then the zone at the gate. J500 has three rules: the zone just left stays loaded and
drawn on an adjacent road (1), an approached zone is full detail before its gate (2), and the far
placeholder is for far zones only (3).

What was wrong, found walking it on a local world rig at Jake's phone layout:

- **Rule 1 kept nothing drawn.** opus-finish's `keepNear` (587b28a4a) kept the departed world resident,
  but `regionalView` hid a region's root whenever no entry was bound, so the road showed the far proxy
  again. A ready resident now has `display(owner)`: its root shows from the road, the cell's coarse
  cover (far proxy and ring tiles) hands over to it as it does inside, and its own lights stay out (the
  road's exterior lighting draws it, warmed first through `warmExteriorRoots`).
- **Rule 1 kept nothing at all for Driftwood.** `retainDeparted` kept a world only while its playing
  cost fit the 1.0 GB envelope; on a phone Driftwood alone plays 1.18 GB, so its region retired 228 ms
  after the gate and walking back in rebuilt it at the gate. A departed world is now kept up to the J410
  1.5 GB line (`DEPARTED_PLAYING_BYTES`); its bound eviction still yields it to an entry.
- **Rule 2 was not built.** `live.ts` built a region only at the gate. `buildAhead` now picks, on the
  road, the nearest zone the walker is heading into whose build time at the walker's speed
  (`readinessModel`, margin 1.25, at least 8 m/s) reaches its gate, and `ensure()`s it; standing still or
  turning away builds nothing, and a ready return waits on no other build.
- **Rule 3 is on by default** (`debug.farNearReach` off only when false), as opus-compat-w12 left ready.

Proof: `scripts/road-reentry-proof.mjs` walks out of a zone's gate (or its portal route, as the J451
smoke does), looks at the zone left and the zones beside the road, walks ahead, turns and walks back
in, sampling the grid every 200 ms with a video and the page's own frame gaps.

Local world rig (this tree's build, headless WebKit at 402x874), Driftwood out its east pier onto the road
toward Nalati, 120 m along, back and in:

- Driftwood drawn from the road on all 220 road samples; Nalati built ahead 12 s after the gate (rule 2)
  and drawn from the road too; the walk back in showed no loading card (0 frames) and entered at once.
- Worst frame gap 507 ms (re-entering Driftwood), 423 ms (the road display's warm-up); no others over
  250 ms.
- Attributed memory (`__wildshard.memory().accountedBytes`): inside Driftwood 718 MB; on the road with
  Driftwood drawn 1.26 GB; with Nalati built ahead too 1.26 GB; back inside 813 MB. Over J445's 1.0 GB
  playing target (a may-crash card, never a refusal), under J410's 1.5 GB hard line: declared here, no cap
  moved.
