# Field water from a plain section

> Written by: Claude

ONE-FORMAT OF40b. Nalati wades its ponds through the native `water.field`
host, which reads the grass field: water stands at the field's level wherever
the pond samples above zero. A plain upload can now say the same with a
`world/field` section (format 0.65.0): the hash of a square f32 pond raster,
its res and size, and the level. The plain server host stands that water when
nothing else installed any; a native product keeps its own. Engine pins
format 0.65.0 for the schema.
