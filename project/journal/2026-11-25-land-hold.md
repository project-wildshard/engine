# Lands refuse an open engine HOLD (opus-kit2, meta QA 11-25 row 37)

- opus-fe13 landed during the 12:56 UTC HOLD: its wait matched `LAND PUSHED` lines and never read HOLD lines.
  `scripts/land-hold.mjs` reads the receipts tail; land.mjs (before the main push) and main-push.mjs (before the
  commit, and again before the push) refuse while an engine HOLD is open, unless the lane is the lander it names.
- One line format, written into the lane rules: `NOTE HOLD engine: <why>; lander <lane>` and `NOTE LIFT engine: <why>`;
  a later `main-watch main green` lifts it too. The 11-25 coordinator lines parse as they are.
- Override (J381): `ALLOW_LAND_DURING_HOLD=1`, named in an outgoing commit body, or it still refuses.
