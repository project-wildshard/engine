# Select the first eligible pursuit ability

> Written by: Codex
> State: verified locally, 2026-11-17.

Declared pursuit exposes its selected target to the authored ability
list. Range, cooldown, positive weight and the creature's own health
gate decide which row is eligible. The host's existing strike runner
owns contact, deadlines and continuation. Legacy single-strike and
score-based callers retain their original cooldown behavior.

Two real shard simulation controls first failed on missing selection.
They now select charge at 12 m and bite at 1.5 m, skip an ineligible
row, apply the authored damage and select a follow-up while bite cools.
A fresh host resumes the live charge with an exact 40-tick continuation.
The focused matrix passed 65 cases across seven files.
