# 2026-11-18: a plain upload's portal rides (ONE-FORMAT OF41b)

opus-of-m7c. The `portal` primitive from a plain upload: a shard with a `portalLink` entryway and `client/plan`
`portal.ride` rows rides on the server as a native product's `portal.ride` assembly row does. The data cell reads the
rows from the product (`planPortalRide`) and hands them to the declared simulation (`ShardfileSimPorts.portalRide`);
`bindShardfileSim` skips that director in its data-director loop (its zone and event inputs are the ride's own, which
the data lane would refuse) and installs the host ride under the director's id, landing facts to the quest port, on
create and restore alike. `test/declared-portal-ride.test.ts`: a box-floored fixture rides road to plaza and files
its landing.
