# MMO login: the door reads join tickets (MMO-PLAN MP2.2c, MP2.2d)

> Written by: Claude (opus-mmo3), 2026-11-17

## MP2.2c: tickets

api-server signs a signed-in player an hour's Ed25519 join ticket,
`ticket:<player>.<device>.<expires>.<signature>`, and the page sends it as
the hello's `player`. The door (`server/host/dedicated/ticket.ts`) checks it
against the pinned public key, with WebCrypto and no call out, so joins keep
working while the api-server is down. A live ticket seats
`account:plr_<player>`, the same on every device, so a phone and a desktop
reach one body. A ticket under another key, or past its expiry, seats the
device it names: that device's guest, as if it had sent its plain id.

The cells never see a ticket: the door re-encodes the hello with the seat
before it forwards it, so a cell's identity check compares like with like,
and the bearer credential goes no further than the door.

The first cut was base64url JSON. The bench found the wire caps hello text
at 255 bytes, which a long device id overran, so the format became dotted
fields and a hex signature, 251 bytes at most. Hex also keeps the no-base64
lint happy.

## MP2.2d: claim

The first time a live ticket seats an account from a device, the device's
saves, receipts and place move to the account (`saves/claim.ts`, one
transaction), but only if the account has no progress yet. A second device's
guest progress is never merged over an account's; it stays that device's.
The move is the "once": afterwards the device has nothing left to move. A
failed claim is logged and the join goes on.

## MP2.2e1: the page's sign-in client

`client/engine/net/playerLogin.ts` holds the page's half: a link asked for and
redeemed (`#login=wsl_…`), passkeys through the browser's WebAuthn JSON
helpers (no base64 on our side; a browser without them is offered none), the
30-day session in the save service's storage, and an hour's ticket per
device. The online identity's `player` is a getter, read at every hello, so
a redial after the 20-minute refresh sends the new ticket. A join waits at
most 3 s for a fresh ticket; with the API down it joins as the device.

## MP2.2e2: the sign-in card

SIGN IN sits beside SETTINGS on the main screen (ACCOUNT once signed in), on
one line on a 402-wide phone; the title-layout proof passes in Chromium and
WebKit. The card (`client/engine/ui/signInCard.ts`) is the Name prompt's glass
card: an email field and Email me a link, a passkey button where the browser
has WebAuthn's JSON helpers, and Google and X behind `SIGN_IN_SWITCHES`, off
until MP2.2f. A mailed link lands as `#login=wsl_…`; the title takes it off
the address first, so a reload never spends it twice, then opens the card on
the answer.
