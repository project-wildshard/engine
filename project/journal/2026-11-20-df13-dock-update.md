# Driftwood dock CPU update

> Written by: Codex

DF13 ranked player prediction, animal and equipment updates, and wire
presentation as the largest steady update systems on the 3× phone-Low
dock. The patch reuses the wire roster until it changes, reads only the
collision fields needed during creature prediction, and reuses the sample
that decides the body’s look when it spawns. Background grid admissions
attach one rejection handler per request. First hints read each obstacle’s
geometry once before writing the out-of-flow overlay. The player still runs
its full fixed-step sweep and can retry a bank where the contacts allow it.

The meter now lists equipment separately from the regional animal system.

Three locked cold runs per build moved median dock update p50 from 4.6 to
4.0 ms at the provisional 3× rate. The five lean slots stay opt-in. DF13
still needs 3.0 ms on the calibrated phone-Low Power bench.
