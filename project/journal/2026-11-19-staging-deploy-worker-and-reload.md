# Follow the staging Worker and the signed client

The v0.1.48 rollout exposed two differences between the harness and the
real release. Doctor must read the uploaded Worker's redirect, then
exercise the same destination in its staging-origin browser. It runs
before the other journeys use the shared socket budget.

The MMO observer keeps the device identities across a reload and uses
the exact signed target client. During the first v0.1.48 attempt both
old clients received reload. The exact target client loaded newer
checkpoints for both identities and kept their ledger and coins. One
progress hash differed between captures. Keep that measurement
inconclusive and retain the hold for a missing or lost save.
