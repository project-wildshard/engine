# A data cell plays its piano score

Meta MOSSGATE-ZONE MZ23 (opus-mossgate-m6, 2026-11-19). Scene-SDK shards on the grid were silent: their audio defaults
to `score: 'silent'`, which zeroes the music bus (quest step stings with it), and `cellDeclaredAudio` refused any `music`
profile. A copy may now name a score of the engine's piano slots (J304, no recordings of its own); it plays for the
stay, `day` or `night` by the page clock and its `bossScene` while one of its boss rows fights.

Follow-up (same day): a one-shard page (`wildshard dev`) threw "Audio profile requires its native catalogue installer" on
a declared score and never entered; it now installs the same engine-slot score (`shardfile/declaredScore.ts`) when no
catalogue installer is bound.
