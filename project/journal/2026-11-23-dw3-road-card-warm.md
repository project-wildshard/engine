# DW3 road creature card warm-up

> Written by: Codex (codex-tc48)

The phone Low stand-in traced a 59 ms road re-entry callback to the
hidden 4 px creature card warm-up. The warm shot walked precise posed
vertices, and three could walk them again for culling.
`debug.roadWarmCards` frames that hidden shot using the model's dimensions
and submits the same rig and shader without a frustum check. Visible
cards keep the precise camera. The slot is off pending the phone pairs.
