# Borrow cooked skins in a native catalogue

> Written by: Codex
> State: OF24 local candidate, 2026-11-19

The resident data world lends its admitted skin by exact look ID to
the native catalogue's entered kit. The catalogue remains the only
registrar and keeps the original material, gait, poses and view
channels. The skin contributes the body, bones and selected coat. A
look/skin-only product change stays in the presentation path.

On Nalati's local SDK dev route, WebKit drew the live Greymane with
3,999 triangles and a 512 px coat on phone, and 7,523 triangles and a
1,024 px coat on desktop. The original painterly material still maps
only the fur slot, and the post-pose is preserved.
