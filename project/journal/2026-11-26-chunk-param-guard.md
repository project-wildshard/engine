# The chunk-param guard (opus-chunk2, CHUNK-PARAM-REMOVAL CP23, J522)

- Jake deleted `?chunk=` (J522). The guard keeps it from coming back: a URL-shaped `chunk` (a query pair after `?`, `&`,
  a quote, a backtick or a parenthesis, or a params call on the string `'chunk'`) in the trees a page, tool or doc is
  made from. Journals and calibration reports are history and stay unread.
- The music sting, Rollup's `type === 'chunk'` and `--chunk=` flags can't match: the pair needs one of those six
  characters right before it, and the call needs a dot and a params method.
- 168 files still carry 369 hits today, while M3 moves the tools and tests and CP22 deletes the engine's reads. They
  sit in a baseline with counts that may only fall, so a lane cleaning a file is never blocked (it gets a `--prune`
  hint) and a lane adding one is.
- The one-shard page's mode branches (15 page-mode comparisons in entry.ts, oneWayIn.ts and pageEntry.ts) print with
  each run: the number the plan's Q2 follow-up starts from.
