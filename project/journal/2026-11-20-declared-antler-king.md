# The Antler King on a declared body

ONE-FORMAT OF23d, engine half. A `boss.ground` roaming fight can now
raise a declared spawn as its boss and stand its thralls from the
declared rows in its spawn group (`pine.king`), instead of the native
roster's parked King. Each stays out of the world until the fight
stands it, leaves again when retired, and a save mid-fight restores
exactly.

The blocker opus-pine5 hit was two things. The King's decision row
never ran: a roster body is observed through the species body port
each tick, a declared one never was, and its fixed step returns early
with no observation. The fight now hands a declared body that same
port once, as its control binds. And three ownership rules assumed a
keeper's decision row never touches a declared spawn: the keeper now
keeps a row a species decision brain names, a borrowed controller may
own a declared spawn with no brain of its own, and admission refuses
only a keeper row over a spawn that has one.

For Pine's half: the King's declared spawn takes the row's actor id
(`pine.antler-king`) and he leaves the runtime's spawns, or the
decision catalogue refuses a duplicate. The guest's actor handles are
compiled from the same sorted registry, so the build keeps them true.
