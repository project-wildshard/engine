## grid-tap-paint: the error modal's cold chunk

`src/client/engine/boot/grid-tap-paint.test.ts` > "RL4 / MF3: a slow boot shows nothing…" went red once in main
ci 43076662972: "expected '' to contain 'failed to start'" after 1016 ms, and passed 3 of 3 alone.

The case's last step is `guardBoot`'s second rejection, which opens the error modal through a lazy
`import('../ui/ErrorModal')` (stuck.ts `showFailure`). That module pulls in the app runtime, strings, terrain and
the error reporter. `vi.waitFor` gives the modal 1 s of real time. On a hosted worker whose transform cache missed,
the import alone took longer, so the modal had not opened when `waitFor` gave up.

Proof: a copy of the old file with a planted 1.2 s (real time) delay on that import fails the same way ("expected
'' to contain 'failed to start'", 1007 ms); the fixed copy passes. The fix loads the modal's module once in the
file's `beforeAll`. The case still goes through the same lazy `import()` and every assertion, and `waitFor` keeps
its 1 s. Stress: 36 of 36 green, cold module cache (`--fsModuleCache=false`), six at a time beside 16 busy loops,
load 20-28 on the 18-core Mac.
