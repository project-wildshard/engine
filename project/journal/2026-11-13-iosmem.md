# 2026-11-13 iosmem: the iPhone killed Driftwood's page

> Written by: Claude (lane opus-iosmem)

Jake's iPhone 17 Pro restarted staging v0.1.16 while Playing on Driftwood (Sentry WILDSHARD-GAME-8G, iOS 18.7,
boot_stage Playing, no breadcrumbs). `scripts/memory-proof.mjs` reads the page at boot, Playing and still: on headless
Chromium as an iPhone the JS heap stands at ~470-490 MB on entering Playing but only ~125 MB of it survives a full
collection, and the renderer process falls from ~1.65 GB to ~1.2 GB as it is collected. A sampling heap profile with
collected objects kept named the road platform's install as the top allocator (~580 MB in the first minute), mostly the
clip's and the bin sort's growing `number[]` lists. They are now growing typed arrays (off the JS heap, no growth
garbage on it). `road-cull-heap.test.ts` holds the live heap per triangle under a budget it failed before.

**PZ24 (opus-pz24): most of the "renderer" was DevTools.** A memory-infra dump (`memory-proof.mjs --dump`) split the
renderer at Playing+10s by allocator. After a full collection of the page and its workers, PartitionAlloc's `buffer`
partition still held 470-530 MB, and the heap snapshot named it: 2,613 `blink::NetworkResourcesData::ResourceData`, the
response bodies Playwright's `Network.enable` keeps for DevTools in the page and in each of the 8 workers. A phone with no
inspector attached never pays that. `memory-proof.mjs` now launches Chromium through `launchMemoryBrowser`
(`scripts/chromium-memory-browser.mjs`, which zeroes those buffers for every session), and the same build reads 1,252 MB
at Playing+10s where it read 1,666-1,852 MB (`buffer` 86 MB after GC, from 527). Every renderer figure taken with the old
launch (opus-iosmem's 1.65 GB, opus-iosmem2's 1,592-1,795 MB) carries that ~420-500 MB. `--inspector-buffers=keep` runs
the old launch for comparison.
