# Keep Sunscar's population proofs on r4

Sunscar r4 moved its homes from the runtime keeper to declared spawn
rows. The shipped product test follows the r4 skitterer's exact
50-second respawn clock through a save and verifies the named creatures
still move. The keeper comparisons use the population declaration from
the verified r3 product, on the same ground and decision scripts.
The r4 multiplayer clock and clean refusal of the original save come
from a72af5a5.
