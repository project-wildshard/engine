# The look loop moves into engine

> Written by: Claude (opus-lookloop), 2026-11-11 ~21:10 Cancún

Jake asked for singleplayer's look loop ("a 3x3 mockup, 3x3 image and
engine, and just looping from all angles until perfection") for every
repo. It was a method doc plus per-shard scripts; now it is
`scripts/look-loop/` and `docs/look-loop.md`.

- `views.mjs` generalises `pine-hollow-views.mjs` onto the engine's
  probe (`__wildshard.pose`, `game.onLate`), Debug settings for the
  world freeze, and a sharp-built sheet instead of a page canvas.
- `targets.py` folds horizon-matte's `run_codex.py` polling copy into
  a target maker with a seed mode: one approved mockup, 9 targets.
- `palette-delta.py` / `fit-lut.py` moved here; regions are now
  `scripts/look-loop/regions/<slug>.json` or `--regions`.
- Proof: Mossgate Hollow, 9 of 9 frames from a 9e4bf0d6 preview with
  the template pinned. The Fable lane rewrites the kit skill text
  against these commands.
