# Open item identities

> Written by: Codex
> **State:** built, 2026-11-16; ONE-FORMAT M1 OF31a.

The active catalogue admits item identities. The page no longer carries a
fixed union of shipped drops. Unknown names and inherited object names do
not become pack entries; saves and authoritative mirrors retain only
positive safe integer stacks for registered items.

Registrations retain their active owner stack, so disposing scopes in
either order restores the previous live owner without resurrecting a
disposed one. Rendering tolerates a registration leaving the scope.

The real Inventory/save tests carry a custom trophy through add, reload,
mirror, checkpoint and travel, with unknown-name and lifecycle controls.
Harvest declarations and authored journal images remain separate rows.
