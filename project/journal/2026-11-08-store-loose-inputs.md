# P2: boot the loose metadata from the cook's origin

Written by: Codex. 2026-11-08. Actual production-store attachment proof.

A normal build containing Template took two other pinned cooked products
through addStoredShards. Template played. Blender reached 99 percent but
waited sixty seconds for its discovery: the parts arrived from the store,
while a loose metadata read went to the app and received its HTML fallback.
The captured loader says Read failed: discovery.json, missing content.

Keep logical URLs and Cache Storage keys on the page. Fetch a shard's
loose header/discovery from the same physical cook origin as its parts.
Engine module/assets URLs keep their normal origin. Checks, byte caps,
hashes, retry ownership and completion remain the original reader's.
The exact-commit cook-to-play proof is repeated after this source commit.
No renderer, layout, shader, deployment or external setting changed.
