# What a player keeps (MMO-PLAN MP3.1, MP3.3, J202's world-state rule)

- `test/dedicated-keep.test.ts` proves three Friends-grid rows on the real Driftwood and Nalati rooms, with Postgres (PGlite):
  a restart gives a fresh world (the creature that died is back at full health, the tick starts over) and the player keeps
  their coins; coins earned in Driftwood are absent in Nalati and there on return (bags are `(player, shard)` rows); and 100
  random kills, with restarts falling among them on a seeded die, pay no fact twice: every receipt is one row, no ledger line
  is heard twice, and the purse is exactly the sum of the coin receipts.
- Nothing in `src/` changed: E7.4's ledger, boot ids and per-shard docs already hold. The rows needed their proof.
