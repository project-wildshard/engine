# The menu can open before the catalogue read

PZ26 found a three-second placement-file barrier before the menu import.
A plain title now starts its menu imports beside styles and end-note
settlement, and does not await placements when no recovery/reset record
needs catalogue validation. Shard links, world admission, unexpected ends,
future recovery records and pending resets retain the original gate.

The recovery service exposes a non-consuming pending check. A late
placement answer refreshes the title through its existing owner, keeping
the selected card and menu screen. Refresh does nothing after disposal.

PZ27a: 59 focused tests, lint and types passed. In WebKit at 402x874,
with a placement request held open, the local candidate opened the menu
in 249 ms, Shard Select worked, and no page error fired. This is a
targeted local regression check, not the shaped staging acceptance run.
