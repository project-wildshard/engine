# 2026-11-14: a painted sky dome (SHARD-SOURCE AP14)

> Written by: Claude (opus-ss4b)

- `render/dataLook.ts` `paintDome`: a look's `look.sky` graph (format 0.5.5, an unlit material over
  `normalize(positionLocal)`) compiles with the backdrop on the page's renderer onto the 600 m dome, back faces, no depth,
  no fog, under the format's `LOOK_SKY_BUDGET` (640 nodes, 1,024 instructions). It replaces the gradient before the
  compose filters `light.environment` from the dome, so the environment and a sky-reflecting pond see the painted sky.
- Proof (sharddev authoring slice, the cleanroom's `makeSky` in TSL): sky bands and clouds sit where the cleanroom's do;
  the pond, now with its surface row, reflects the violet sky. Sheet in
  `~/.cache/wildshard/plan-artifacts/shard-source-stage4/img/ap14-sky-sheet.jpg`.
- `paintDome` takes the compile function, so its test runs the classic compiler with no page renderer and no module mock.
