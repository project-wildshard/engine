> Written by: Codex
>
> **State:** engine preparation, 2026-11-19. IP11 awaits IP7 and IP9.

Register iPhone14,5 in phone-model.json as a report-only device.

have named null entries. Alpha, scale and the limits are unmeasured.

The fitter prints the entry with --device=iPhone14,5 and carries
it into a 17 Pro refit. measure.mjs reports the pending fit beside
the existing EM11 reading. The 17 Pro calibration and gate inputs
keep their original values. The Nine error-bar check waits for IP9.
