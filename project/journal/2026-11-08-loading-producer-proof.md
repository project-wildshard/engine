unearned initial percentage. A negative fixture reproduced that false
green. The checker now recomputes each phase's exact numeric share from
its relevant producer and denominator, including the initial reading.

timeline and selected download operation. No polling, event copies or
extra scheduling is added to boot. The manual recorder forwards each

attributes and its presentation snapshot no longer prove its own bar.
