# Versioned staging uses the cooked store throughout

Connection Doctor still probed the legacy server book, and live
placement catch-up asked for its old per-build cook index. Those reads
kept staging tied to the old pack host after the PV25 room switch.

A signed cook tuple sends Doctor to the cooked road and a versioned
room pack. Zone-table pages resolve fresh placements from the same
cook and require the raw hash in the signed placement. A verified
placement replaces its affected baked addresses after the baked read
settles, while the page keeps its existing boot deadline.
