# Give declared firearms an aim view

Brasscap's three firearms were receiving the AIM latch, but their
shared declared lever family read neither it nor the desktop aim
hold. Aim now narrows the camera, moves the front sight toward its
centre and slows look in both the grid and standalone play. Release
or a swap restores the lens and look speed. The server's firing law
and the weapon rows stay as authored.

QA feedback 2026-11-19 row 39. Tests cover the lever-action, scattergun
and long rifle, touch aim, desktop hold and restoring the hip view.
