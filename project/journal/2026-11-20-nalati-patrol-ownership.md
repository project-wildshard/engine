# Let the patrol own its room

Patrol riders use the shared admitted species recipe reader. The patrol
still reserves its original tick before bodies move. If a room has the
Titan, it finishes the patrol before installing the arena. A room without
the Titan finishes its own patrol, Captain and Wind Elite.

In the packed Nalati room with the arena host omitted, the natural
night produced three riders at tick 24,558. Their save and effects
matched for 120 ticks after restore. The Captain still required the
real kill threshold.
