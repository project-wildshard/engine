# Walk the staging zone edges

J451 makes the post-deploy phone WebKit walk part of the release
decision. A guest walks from Driftwood to Sky Reach, then uses the
live grid's shared sides for a continuous tour. Each arrival checks
drawn play and movement. The deploy verdict names the failed pair.
Production requires the exact build's complete result.

The first staging v0.1.50 run found 96 directed crossings and failed
on Driftwood into Sky Reach. The page set gameplay ready, and the
world region still showed the page background in three play frames.
Draw recovered in the full-window run. The player remained stationary
with walk input throughout the 90-second entry window.
The tour and its TypeScript fixture share the same declared cell shape.
