Port the dedicated builder, pack-origin writer, socket/room tools and
VPS operational inputs. Nothing was deployed. Build and proof scratch
live outside the workspace under the lane's lift-work directory.

room-deploy first failed because it still looked for release.kept;
the current startup reports release.installed. Check the actual
installed serial 1 against rejected newest serial 2, not an obsolete
event name. Keep every gameplay, hash, save, telemetry and crash check.

The corrected local proof passes on real PostgreSQL. Two bots return
0.04 m from their saved stands after restart and release change.
Restart took 2701 ms and the good release took 6434 ms, including its
3000 ms grace and 2000 ms supervisor delay. Four walks each exceed
7.29 m. One room record contains 599 ticks. Saves have zero failures,
and a missing-pack worker exits 1 after sending its fatal report.

The release schema, puller and every local release publisher require
or emit saveEpoch 1 or greater. Never skip a hook to publish this work.
