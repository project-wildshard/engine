# The King's shared body recipe

> Written by: Codex
>
> **State:** OF24, 2026-11-20.

The arena room can read the King from the cooked creature book. The
body ID, health, dimensions, attack defaults and pose stay the same.
The room's attack program supplies damage. The generic species charge
damage would change the arena defaults if copied to the room body.

Older products still supply their server species file. The pinned
Nalati test compares both prepared body recipes exactly and refuses
an undeclared variant. A local shard cook omits the redundant file.
