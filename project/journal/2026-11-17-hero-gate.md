# Placed zones wear their uploaded hero (opus-hero1)

Jake, 2026-11-17 ~14:32 Cancún: "in zone select there are zones without hero images that's bad". Every shard the API
placed after the build wore the plain `NO_ART` card (`catalogueTitleRow`), though the cook copies each upload's discovery
document and its card's images into `loose/<hash>/shardfiles/<slug>/`. Glowfen and Mirror Salt carried real heroes that
nobody saw. Meta HERO-GATE HG4.

`placedContent` now reads that discovery document when the cook's index lists it, and takes its card when all three
images are blob paths of the same slug that the index also lists. `learnPlacedCells` puts that card on the row. A placed
slug with no hero (none, the generated `data:` card, an image the cook did not copy) keeps its cell and `?chunk=`, but
its card is `hidden` (Developer only), so a player never sees a zone without its picture. Upload (sharddev) and the
API's commit now refuse a shard file with no hero, so this is for uploads made before the gates.
