# OF23: Pine's species as rows (opus-of23)

- OF23a engine half: the creature layer's hunt motion takes any subset of
  its five numbers (Pine's deer and elk author walk and trample only); an
  absent one keeps the hunt's default, which for walk is not one number.
- OF23b: `test/native-roster-declared-creatures.test.ts` proves declared
  spawns beside Pine's `population.roster` (OF22b0's path): Pine's deer and
  boar as rows stand, tick, save and restore exactly; a roster id refuses.
- OF23c (opus-sdk-pine): a level's declared `creature:N` bodies keep their
  ids and the population roster's own ids start past the highest of them.
  Pine's herds become declared rows with r6's ids (creature:0 to 160); the
  roster then hands the King's and thralls' reserves 161 to 163 and the
  elites 164 to 167, as r6 did. The OF23b refusal case now reads as the
  roster diverging from a bake that still rolls that id (it failed first,
  expecting the old "one body, one owner"). Proven on Pine's OF23c build:
  the boot census equals r6's body for body.
