# The box cook gets 7 CPUs and 8G (FAST-RELEASE FR-A22)

- opus-cook: the box cooked Pine in 849 s against 258 s on the MacBook. Profile (read-only on the box): 16-core AMD EPYC
  Genoa, load 0.12; the cook unit sat on AllowedCPUs=13-15, so COOK_CPU_SLOTS 3 gave the ASTC encoder 2 lanes, and the
  textures phase took 810 of 820 s. The MacBook passes no slots and runs MAX_LANES 6. No GPU or WebKit in the cook job:
  it is all WebAssembly encode on the CPU.
- rebuild.sh: the cook takes 9-15 (staging's 9-12 too) with CPUWeight=20 and Nice=10, so staging rooms and Postgres win
  contention; production's 0-8 untouched. harden: cook MemoryMax 5G -> 8G (Pine's 6-lane job peaked at 3963 MB on the
  MacBook; the service already peaked at 4.24G with 2 lanes).
- Expected: a box core is about 1.7x a MacBook core here (810 s x 2 lanes vs 157 s x 6), so Pine about 280 s, 1.1x.
