actor attack/death timers and damage time choose the frame; no playback clock,
controller, body or RNG is added. The sampled locals are lent directly to the
bound skin and grid data view, so their drawn joints and judged volumes agree.

the ordinary presentation flinch/terrain root tilt. Presentation-only pose
layers refuse explicitly for clip-hit rows rather than being silently ignored.

bounded historical endpoints record clip poses for rewind; native histories
retain their exact old root-only allocation. Restoration uses the existing
host/actor clocks and complete immutable body/skin source fence.
