- CC30a: format main `e0b80687` (body `attachments`, a volume's break
  `channel`) rides the 0.37.1 patch like CC28a's hunks. Drop it with the
  other backports when QA29 moves the pin past it.

  at the volume's break channel, so a broken plate is a 1 on wire 1.1's
  channels and every page sees it with no new field.

  opened to any `Animal`: a group per attachment on its bone, a mesh per
  part. `clientAttachments.ts` resolves the body's sockets, leases the
  GLBs through the item models' loader (now `loadStaticModels`) and drops
  `desktop` ones on the phone tier; the page reads `when` and the break
  channel off the row's channels every frame.

  in the worktree: commit before `land`.
