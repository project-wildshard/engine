# 2026-11-13 pagesize: the plan's open rows in one branch

> Written by: Claude (lane opus-pagesize)

**PZ24, the page's own road textures.** A WebGL call probe on staging (`cdce05f1`, iPhone profile, phone tier, Driftwood)
named the textures behind the ledger's 76 MB: the road look is built twice (A1's ring-first halves), and each half painted
and uploaded its own asphalt (1024x512) and junction (1024x1024) canvas, and its own sign atlas (1024x4096 and 1024x6464
RG8, both with mips, 28.8 MB together; the second half's textures read as owner `level`). The phone tier now paints the
sign atlas at half size, the same commands under one transform (a quarter of the texels), and the halves share one
asphalt and one junction upload through a counted `RoadMapShare`. The rest of the list is the composer's RGBA16F colour
buffer (804x1748, 11.2 MB), the shadow map's 1024 colour and depth (8.4 MB; three 0.186 gives a PCF map a colour texture
it never samples) and Driftwood's terrain AO (an RGBA 1024 with mips for one channel, 5.6 MB, shard content).

**PZ18, the title in the first wave.** `scripts/app-build/titlePreloads.mjs` runs after the chunk report and adds a
`modulepreload` for each chunk of `grid/pageEntry`'s static closure the document does not already name (on this tree:
`title-0-c-…` and `title-0-s-…`), after the bundler's own four. `test/title-preloads.test.ts` pins the selection and placement.

**PZ19, the hero stops outrunning the code.** The shell now preloads and paints a ~30 kB first cut of each hero
(`main-crossroads-square-first.jpg` 520 px q45, 30.1 kB; `main-crossroads-landscape-first.jpg` 640x360 q50, 28.5 kB, from
the 232 kB and 172 kB originals with ImageMagick). The menu layers the full image over its first cut
(`--ws-hero-square: url(full), url(first)`), so the full image is fetched only once the title is up and the art never blanks.

**PZ12, isolation per channel (J274, preview first).** `scripts/app-build/isolation.mjs` holds the isolated channels
(preview only) and appends one `/*` rule with COOP `same-origin` and COEP `require-corp` to an isolated build's
`dist/_headers`, so the document, its module workers and the standalone worker's script all carry them. The service
worker reads that rule and sends the same two on its baked document (`__DOCUMENT_HEADERS__`), and an isolation change
is a new worker id. `standalone-smoke.mjs` serves a build with its own rule and expects isolation exactly when the rule
isolates. No channel sent either header before (preview's `/` had neither).
