# 2026-11-23 DW3: a steady 30 at phone Low (mini-dw3)

- `route-bench --leg-census`: every leg samples every tenth drawn frame and prints the owners of its heaviest tenth (the
  west path p95 view, which the end-of-route census never saw).
- `debug.roadPoolSliced` (off): after the road handoff, every tile publish/retire re-merged the material pool at once
  (phone plateau climb: 4-5 frames of 51-72 ms, update 22-34 ms + 3.2 MB upload). Sliced, the pool stays deferred,
  the backing advances merges in one shared 4 ms slice a step, and parent masks wait for the merge that holds the tiles.
- `writeIndexTriangles`: a turned CPU-backed index uploads only the written runs (addUpdateRange); the phone re-sent
  props.merged's 3.4 MB index in a 38 ms draw on the west path at a far-copy swap.
- `debug.coverReachLow` / `debug.coverKeepLow` (unset = authored): the phone west legs draw 525-606k cover triangles
  (leg census by kind); phone Low can scale the cover reach and thin far hand-overs, for the phone pairs and a J430 board.
