# 2026-11-23 DW3: a steady 30 at phone Low (mini-dw3)

- `route-bench --leg-census`: every leg samples every tenth drawn frame and prints the owners of its heaviest tenth (the
  west path p95 view, which the end-of-route census never saw).
- `debug.roadPoolSliced` (off): after the road handoff, every tile publish/retire re-merged the material pool at once
  (phone plateau climb: 4-5 frames of 51-72 ms, update 22-34 ms + 3.2 MB upload). Sliced, the pool stays deferred,
  the backing advances merges in one shared 4 ms slice a step, and parent masks wait for the merge that holds the tiles.
- `writeIndexTriangles`: a turned CPU-backed index uploads only the written runs (addUpdateRange); the phone re-sent
  props.merged's 3.4 MB index in a 38 ms draw on the west path at a far-copy swap.
- `debug.coverReachLow` / `debug.coverKeepLow` (unset = authored): the phone west legs draw 525-606k cover triangles
  (leg census by kind); phone Low can scale the cover reach and thin far hand-overs, for the phone pairs and a J430 board.
- roadPoolSliced r2: the first phone ABBA was noisy at the dock (on 20/15 frames >50, off 5); a sliced merge now lands
  through publishes (only a retirement of a tile it reads restarts it), masks wait by revision.
- route-bench --leg-alloc: per leg, the new typed-array storage and its call sites. Phone fulls come every ~23 s and
  each is a >50 ms frame; JSC counts buffer bytes toward the next full, so this names who feeds them.
- debug.roadPoolArena: road pool merges fill one of two reused RoadTileArenas (the one the drawn mesh does not hold).
  --leg-alloc on desktop WebKit: 1.4 GB of typed arrays over the route, ~0.48 GB these merged arrays (GPU-only after
  upload, so garbage a frame later). The culler draw index stays its own: gpuOnly copies an index viewing a bigger buffer.
- route-bench --leg-vsync: drawn-frame cadence by rAF timestamp beside the game clock. Phone slow frames (40-48 ms)
  carry update p95 9 and render p95 11 and pair with 24-31 ms ones: a late callback, not a late present?

- route-bench --heap-at <leg> (DF18): the phone's fulls cost 65-190 ms every 22-32 s and no typed-array cut moved them.
  The pause follows the marked object graph, so sum JSC's own live heap by class after a leg (Heap.snapshot).
