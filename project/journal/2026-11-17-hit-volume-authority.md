# Authoritative hit parts

> Written by: Codex
> State: CC6c1 source, 2026-11-17.

Explicit body layers read their actual admitted skin's inverse binds.
The same position-only bind contract as clientSkins feeds root-moved
rest volumes, without a renderer, second actor/controller or pose clock.
Variant skins retain their exact geometry. The source/rig identity is
an immutable continuation adapter; changed or missing authority refuses.

Native ray/nearest-skin queries read the compiled volumes and return
their part and multiplier. Rewind carries their points to the old root
and their contact back to the living root. The damage pipeline rejects
zero multipliers before callbacks/events; declared rifles and native
ranged adapters use the authored bonus instead of the legacy head bonus.
Omitted bodies retain their old query, publication, broad phase and save.

The actual declared rifle command on a GLB-backed biped deals 20 on the
head, 10 on the body and zero on its shield, with named damage outcomes.
Real current/source-mismatch/missing-source restore and rewind controls
pass. Registry format 0.19.0 is pinned, with 17 cases in six files,
including the original rifle/projectile/sweep/javelin/polearm controls.
The rest assertion compares canonical saved coordinates (JSON treats
negative zero as zero); native bit/fore checks remain separate and exact.

Fallback correction: existing procedural looks and own-rig skins with
no authored hit/socket overrides keep dims/fore and install no adapter.
Two real-sim controls compare their complete saves to the same rows
without a body layer, before and after 30 ticks. The combined real
physics/rifle/local sword/projectile/native combat matrix passes
35 cases in four files, with full types.
