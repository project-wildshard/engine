# DF5: keep the GPU fit honest

> Written by: Codex
>
> **State:** checker built, route qualification pending, 2026-11-20.

The old dock pair varied 4.5 times on the phone. A ratio from one of those
readings would make the table look precise without predicting another
segment. `scripts/fps/gpu-fit.mjs` takes a manifest of three independent
phone/Mac routes, fits only declared training segments, and tests each
held-out segment in every run against the 25% line. It records each
segment's ratios and their spread even when qualification fails.

The checker refuses repeated captures, incomplete routes, mixed builds,
different renderers, raster mismatches and different graphics presets.
It produces an unqualified result when even one held-out run misses.
The floor table can keep its GPU estimate blank until the fit qualifies.
