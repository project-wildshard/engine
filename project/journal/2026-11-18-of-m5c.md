# ONE-FORMAT M5, third owner (opus-of-m5c)

> Written by: Claude.

**CC28a (actor channels on the wire).** Wire 1.1.0 adds animal fields 25
to 29: four actor channels and the lock answer. The engine binds them from
`AnimalSim.channels` (initial values from the body's declared `channels`)
and `lockOverride`, and a page's `showRow` writes both back, so a lock and
Tab on a remote page agree with CC32's `Do.lockable`. A script sets a
channel with the format's new ABI-1 command `actorChannel` (28); the value
rides the decision continuation like CC32's locks, so body snapshots are
unchanged. Format main `16b19037`/`5afdb478` is unreleased and rides the
0.37.1 patch (drop with the other backports when QA29 moves the pin). The
cross tests now want each local animal column by id, since a HEAD client
has five columns the released wire lacks.

**CC28b (channel looks).** A body's `bind` reaches its page look as
`SpeciesDef.channelLook` (channel names turned into wire indices), and
each Animal's own body material follows its channels every frame: tint
lerps the material colour, emissive adds to the resting glow the hit
flash fades back to, and opacity is the channel's value (transparent only
below 1). The look switch by channel (`swap`) is CC28c: it needs the
second look registered under a page kind of its own and a respawn of the
row's body.

**CC28c (look swap).** A body's `swap` look registers on the page under
its own kind (`<look>~swap`) with a copy of its species row, and never
under the species' kind, where two looks would contend (the species
service takes a kind's last look). `wirePage.lookKind` says which kind a
row draws as from its channels; a row whose channel crosses `at` has its
body retired, and the next sync spawns it in the other look.
