# Chat and mic only in play

> Written by: Claude (opus-loadhud), 2026-11-11

Jake on his iPhone, ~21:06 Cancún, staging v0.1.9: "lol don't show microphone and chat on the loading screen". The online
corner (input, mic, first-time mic tip) mounts when the online shell starts, which is under the loading card, and the only
thing that hid it was an open menu (`data-ws-menu`). On the dev-hold READY / PLAY card it sat over the rows and SETTINGS.

The rule is now one pure function, `cornerShown` in `src/client/host/online/chatCorner.ts`, over the page's marks on
`<html>`: the app state must be an in-world one (play, explore, practice, playground, dead), with no menu and no loading
card. The card needed a new mark, `data-ws-covered` from `UiLayers.holdCovered`, because `finish.ts` sets the state to
`play` before the card leaves. A MutationObserver follows the marks; no timers decide it. B / the talk key follow the same
rule, and the first-time mic tip's 15 s now start when the corner first shows instead of burning under the card.
