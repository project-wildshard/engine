Jake's five-wave grill settled the phone HUD: chat and mic become two icons stacked above MOVE, the talk box sits above
the thumbs, quest news gets one toast slot, and the right thumb (FIRE, AIM, DODGE, JUMP) and the move stick stay as
they are. Portrait only (Jake ~17:34: "Landscape doesnt exist btw lol"). One entry per row, newest last.

- hud-worst: the chat bundle had stopped building (rapier's top-level await in an IIFE); a pre plugin stubs rapier and
  keeps lazy imports external, so the full-mode shots work again.
- Row 1: the closed corner on a touch HUD is the mic over a chat icon. The input stays in the DOM, invisible, so a tap
  on the icon focuses it inside the tap's gesture and iOS raises the keyboard. The badge counts lines others said while
  the chat was closed, 9+ at most, and clears on open. A desktop keeps its input row.
- Row 2: the closed corner on a phone peeks only its newest line, one line beside the chat icon, for 4 s (PEEK_MS),
  then the badge alone. "In a fight" is new: a hit taken or landed in the last 8 s (`fightMark.ts`), marked on `<html>`
  as `data-ws-fight` (no peek then) plus a `data-ws-hurt` count for row 3's close-on-a-hit and row 8's HP rule.
- Row 3: the keyboard dock (MK2) grows to 190 pt and holds the input and the last 6 lines. On a phone a send closes the
  chat and drops the keyboard (the desktop keeps chatting mode), and a hit taken (`data-ws-hurt` moving) closes it.
- Row 4: the right-thumb discs vanished in a talk because any modal pushed the `menu` input context (blocks below).
  A dialogue box with no menu over it now takes the game's `dialog` context, which blocks nothing, so FIRE, AIM, DODGE
  and JUMP stay live and the stick can walk off; firing closes the box. On a phone the box sits above the disc row,
  12 px gutters, 17 px text kept at three lines, long lines split into pages (`talkPages.ts`, measured in the box's
  font), NEXT and LEAVE as footer buttons and no tap-the-box. Nothing about the discs themselves changed.
- Row 5: on a touch HUD the toast column becomes one slot at the top centre, stepped under the status column, the
  tracker and the map by ToastStack's obstacle pass. One toast at a time for 2.5 s, three held at most (the oldest
  waiting one goes), and plain news said within 250 ms of the last (STEP COMPLETE and the next OBJECTIVE come in one
  call) joins it with a ▸. The keyboard page keeps its right-hand column.
- Row 6: most of the tracker rule already held (one line, ellipsis on the name, count and distance never shrink, a
  yielding chip gone at once since QA 11-19 row 28). On a phone it is now at most 60vw and clips at its own edge.
- Row 7: a quest's own world pins carry `data-tracked`; on a phone they show the diamond and the distance only, since
  the tracker names the goal. Other pins keep their names.

  hide it on a local build; Brasscap shows it because the lever-action is ranged. No fix there. The phone HP strip now
