# The stranger's hard cases (ONE-FORMAT AC6)

- A script's `despawn` [own actor, the owned actor that takes its place or 0] holds a form out of the world (hidden,
  not alive, deep under it: no death, credit or loot) and stands the other form where it stood, facing its way, at the
  same share of its own max hp. The held forms are the decision continuation's `absent`. This is Q-CC48 (b): the
  shape-shifter whose skeleton changes. `test/script-despawn.test.ts`.
- A scripted strike its species also lists as an ability keeps the ability's knockback and projectile, as the same
  strike does under a brain: the stranger mech's stomp and rockets run from its script. `test/script-strike-abilities.test.ts`.
