# Shared cooked storm inputs

> **State:** OF24 compatibility, 2026-11-20.
> Written by: Codex

The native storm can read the shelter circles and policy from
`world/forest-weather.json`. The ordinary held-weapon owner can name
that same policy path in its assembly input. Older products retain the
original server-file inputs and the storm's position in install order.

Nalati's local packed build dropped two duplicate files. Its original
nine shelter circles, active storm save and emitted effects matched
through tick 185. The sealed product passed the patrol and weapon verb
tests. The weather owner still borrows the native riding environment.
