# Spent use prompts (TE-ENG6)

> Written by: Claude

Mossgate's "Light brazier A" stayed up after A was lit, and Brasscap's "Take the lever-action" after the take: a USE band
across a seventh of a phone screen, three lanes saw it. `installDeclaredTargets` now takes a `spent` rule
(`spentUses`): a declared use leaves the prompt list while its row's `unless` condition (shardfile `1733591`, a
`hooks.conditions` id) holds, or while the kit.lever its scene takes is in this player's hand. It comes back when that
stops (a reset puts A out). Rows that name neither are never spent, so native and older shards keep every prompt. The
server also projects a lever's take to a player field when `itemFields` asks (`property: "taken"`), so a panel can hide
the rack's own rifle (BM-3's follow-up; Brasscap needs that panel and field in its rows). Not here: a grid template copy's
prompts (`copyPrompts`) still show spent uses.
