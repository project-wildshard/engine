# Zone Select at the room door
> State: implemented, 2026-11-19.
> Written by: Codex

Each room server answers GET /zone-select for its own channel under J470.
Featured comes from engine source. Popular sorts 30 days of confirmed human
arrivals. New sorts first approved uploads and applies the listing flag.
All exposes the room's complete list for the Developer tab. Live cell
availability is checked on each read. Upload metadata and aggregate arrivals
are refreshed together per process and retain their latest successful reads.
