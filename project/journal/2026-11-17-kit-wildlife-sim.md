# Exercise Kit wildlife in the authored simulation

> Written by: Codex
> State: verified locally, 2026-11-17.

The frozen plain deer and wolf rows, GLBs and skin files come from
sharddev a2228a82. The build tests cover plain files and public helpers
producing the same products. Engine tests load those exact bytes so
hosted runs can repeat the proof without a sibling checkout.

A live Kit hind bolts and creates space without damaging the player.
Damage to the Kit wolf calls the same-pack wolf inside the assist
radius. The other pack and the far member stay out of pursuit.
