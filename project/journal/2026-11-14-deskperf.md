# Desktop frame rate (opus-deskperf, NB5 widened)

Jake, 2026-11-14 ~00:00 Cancún, desktop Chrome on his Mac, prod v0.1.18: Nine Dragon 49 fps down the street, Nalati 30,
Driftwood 40-70 (meta QA-FEEDBACK-2026-11-14 rows 2, 3, 5). His window is 1496x717 CSS at dpr 2.

- `scripts/loading-flow/deskbench.mjs`: his window, desktop tier, uncapped headless Metal, the title's route into each
  zone; per pose the drawn frame, update / render CPU, draws, buffer; `--ab` interleaves levers against the baseline so
  this busy Mac's drift (load 18-39 from other lanes) cancels; `--census`, `--profile`.
- Measured on main `8b1563bc`, his window: Nine ~80-125 fps, Nalati ~70-100, Driftwood ~75-90 at spawn. The frame is
  ~80 % main-thread work; render scale 1.5 to 1.0 saves 0-1 ms. The same run swings 2x with the machine's load. ANGLE
  Metal timer queries read 17-24 ms for 7-10 ms frames: not usable.
- Levers by interleaved A/B: shadow maps held saves 1.3-2.0 ms on Nalati / Driftwood (~90-110 of their ~250-320
  draws), nothing on Nine (its maps are not redrawn per frame); AO off saves 0.6-2.4 ms.
- Built: desktop takes the phone's staggered cascades (BA-3's near / 2nd / 4th cadence); `renderBudget.ts`, a desktop
  pixel budget (4.2 Mpx) and dynamic resolution on the drawn interval that takes back a step that does not pay.
- deskbench `--walk N` (drawn intervals plus long animation frames while walking) for the 246 ms crowd spike, and
  `--extra` poses along the spawn's facing for his street view and the skyline hole.
- Reproduced his frame: one other uncapped WebGL page on the GPU (a stand-in for the agents' headless benches running at
  00:00) takes Nine on main to 41-42 fps, frame 23.6-24.2 / 34.1-34.6 ms (his shot: 49 fps, 22.5 / 34.8 ms) and
  Driftwood to 41-43. With the budget, under the same load: Nine 49-64 fps at ratio 1, Driftwood 45-52 at 1-1.125. Render
  CPU time is the frame there (15-20 ms): the draw calls wait on a GPU the other page holds.
- The skyline hole is not a neighbouring cell's far view: hiding every region scene (`--hide '^region-scene:'`) leaves
  it. It shows on staging v0.1.18 and main alike, above the street facing away from the spawn: Nine's own night sky or
  backdrop behind its hazed buildings. Not fixed here.
