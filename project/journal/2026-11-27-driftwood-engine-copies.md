# Driftwood's last engine copies leave engine git (SF24 red)

SF24 pinned Driftwood at its images-only upload 14 (engine 76229e4e9).
Driftwood now plays its own island set and carries its own lightmaps and
captain, so six tracked files under `public/` lost their only owner and
main's `ci` went red on `test/public-verdicts.test.ts` (run 46023033901,
"every file under public/ is on a list or a zone's").

- The two piano tracks (`island-calm`, `island-tension`) go on
  `public-engine.json`: engine music, as the SF10 to SF12 batch lists
  them.
- The four Driftwood model files (`driftwood-blender` lm-ao, lm-bounce and
  its phone copy, the `driftwood-hero` captain GLB) leave engine git, as
  SF13 did for Driftwood's other unread lm-ao copies and as the batch's
  0004 patch does. Nothing in engine source names them; only the
  generated byte table (untracked) and a URL string in
  `ship-zone-entry.test.ts` did.

Proof: with all 27 pins installed, `test/public-verdicts.test.ts` passes
6 of 6 and `test/ship-zone-entry.test.ts` passes. Guard: the verdict test
itself, which is what caught it.
