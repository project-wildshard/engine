7 by 7 off screen, and Jake said at 17:03 that 0.25x shows the whole 7 by 7. So 0.25x is the map's zoom 1, the grid edge to
edge across the width. The stops live in mapView.ts (`gridStops`); the menu builds its chips from `FullMap.stops`, so a
one-shard map or a practice room keeps its old 1x · 2x · 4x.

A pinch now settles on the nearest stop, on a log scale, when the fingers lift. It eases there over 160 ms about the
pinch's midpoint, so the lit chip always says where you are. The wheel stays smooth, as the board says. Pan and the
J455 clamp are unchanged (Jake approved them at 17:12).

The header and the full width apply to the BAG only (`.ws-gmenu:not(.pause)`). PAUSE / SETTINGS keep their bar. The
CLOSE button is drawn 34 px tall but its tap area stays 44 px, past the button. On a phone the "Driftwood Isle" line over
the map is hidden, because the header's subtitle already names the zone (the board counted it three times).
