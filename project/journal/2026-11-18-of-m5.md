# ONE-FORMAT M5: the custom hatch (opus-of-m5b)

- CC59h: `test/fixtures/decisionHarness.ts` runs a few lines of AssemblyScript as a real ABI-1 guest through
  `ShardDecisions` on a two-boar shardfile, so every `Do.*` verb row starts with a failing test. Guests stay frozen
  pins (no compiler in tests); `WS_FREEZE_GUESTS=1 WS_SHARDDEV=<sharddev>` pins a missing one with sharddev's real
  compiler via `scripts/freeze-script-guest.mjs`. Gotcha: a call's commands commit on the tick after the call, so
  `decide()` steps to the next 6th tick and one more.
- CC32: four decision commands (format `8460098a`, backported onto the 0.37.1 pin): `lockable` sets
  `AnimalSim.lockOverride` (saved in the decision continuation as optional `locks`, so body snapshots are unchanged);
  `heal` clamps at max hp; `pull` is `PlayerSim.pullToward`, the knockback law aimed from the far side; `root` is the new
  `effect.root` status (moveLocked override, `status-root` icon) for the script's time. Pull and root refuse on an
  embedded host, whose page owns the player's motion. `test/creature-verbs.test.ts` drives each through the harness.
- CC59b: brain hooks. A `{ brain: <spawn> }` decision row rides a creature its brain (spawn or species temperament)
  still drives: no strike catalogue swap, no stop on failure. Hit events carry the row's `tags` mask in their last
  slot; `creature.part-broken` (now with the volume index) reaches the hook as event 19; `ability` sets
  `AnimalSim.nextAbility`, which `prepareAbilities.request` tries before its own pick (kept until in range, saved as the
  continuation's optional `abilities`). ShardDecisions now runs at the platform fuel ceilings (2M a call, 8M a tick);
  a guest past them is refused and after three refusals stopped and reported while the brain fights on.

## CC59c: phase adds and `spawn`

An encounter phase's `adds` (format `1918b0d5`, backported onto the 0.37.1 pin) are spawn rows that wait out of the
world, as an absent `when` row does, until their encounter fights at that phase (its beat included), or a decision's
`spawn` command (5) calls one, or a group of them, out early. A fight that resets (armed or dormant) sends the adds
away again. `encounterAdds.ts` keeps waiting and called adds as one adapter, installed only when some phase lists adds,
so older saves restore. `spawn` is admitted only when the shard has adds; despawn, impulse and weather stay refused.
