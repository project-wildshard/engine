# HUD tidy and two QA rows (opus-hud, 2026-11-24)

> Written by: Claude

Lane opus-hud: meta `docs/plans/HUD-TIDY-PLAN.md` (HT1, HT2) and meta QA-FEEDBACK-2026-11-24 rows 19 and 36. Jake was firm
that nothing else on the HUD changes: the right thumb and the move stick stay put.

## Row 19: the Bag map's zone names

Jake's iPhone shots at fit showed NINE DRAGON STACK and PASTEL ALIEN PLAIN turned sideways and running down across the
neighbouring tiles. Those were the Rims A sideways names (QA 11-23-b row 16), meant for a thin strip at the frame's side,
firing inside the frame on the overview. He asked for no names at fit and 0.25x at all. `namesZones` (mapView.ts) draws the
zones' names from the log midpoint of 0.25x and 0.5x up, and `placeInside` no longer turns a name: a rim too thin for an
upright name leaves it unnamed. The fit still keeps every line inside its own tile.

## Row 36: the ping chip

The rooms link already timed its own pings (`WebSocketTransport.stats.rttMs`), but online.js is a bundle of its own, so the
page can't share a module with it. The link now says its round trip once a second as a `ws:rooms-ping` document event
(`net/roomsPing.ts`), and `ui/pingChip.ts` shows it on the Developer dock after the fps pill and the memory card, "--" when no
word came for 2.5 s (offline, a preview, a dropped link). The memory chip drops to one decimal; its panel keeps two. At 402 x
874 with a 3-digit ping the row still ends well before the minimap (hud-worst --check, WebKit and Chromium).

## HT1 and HT2: the storm badge and the pickup line

Both moments now go to the one toast slot a touch HUD already had (J473 row 5: one line, 2.5 s each, a queue of 3). GET LOW
is `ToastStack.pin`: it takes the slot at once, holds while the lightning is near, and the line it cut short comes back
first. A pickup is a `pickup` toast ("PICKED UP · SEA GLASS · CHARM · 5"), and the line up carries "+N" for those waiting.
The first shots showed the slot sitting between the tracker and the boss bar when no kill feed pushed it down; on a touch
HUD the slot now always sits under a shown boss bar, as both mocks draw it. The storm chip is mounted inside the minimap on a
touch HUD and pinned to its bottom edge, so it follows the minimap's real size (the grid page's minimap is not the 27.2vw
the CSS comment says, and a CSS-only badge missed the edge by 7 px and drew under the rim). Keyboard pages keep the big card
and the top-centre warning. `hud-worst.mjs --tidy` shoots both moments and fails on any toast or badge over the boss bar, the
kill feed, the quest chip or the minimap; `--map` shoots the Bag map at each zoom chip.
