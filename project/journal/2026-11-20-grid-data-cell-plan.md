> Written by: Claude (opus-drift6)

showed the city half built: a gate and dark boxes where the one-shard page has lanterns, stalls, crowds and neon. A data
cell has no runtime context of its own, but the grid page's context draws in the home cell's frame, so the entered home
data cell now installs its remaining plan rows there (`grid/dataCellPlan.ts`), phase by phase, for as long as the feet
stay. A local staging-snapshot grid on Nine drew the lanterns, the portal ring and the crowd, and the claw still zips.

follow-up.
