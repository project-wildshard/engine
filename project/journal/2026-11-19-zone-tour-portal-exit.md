# Let the zone-entry tour leave portal arrivals

On staging v0.1.52, Far Reach's gate into Nine reaches Lantern Square
through Nine's west deck portal. The walker then ran west into a wall
for two minutes. The declared route leads back through the square's
portal to the west deck, where a player can step onto the road.

Read the served shard's entryway routes for inland arrivals. Follow
that path using movement input and start the road leg at the observed
deck. Use the hoverboard only for the long straight public roads.
Walk the turn-ins and cross the gates on foot. Keep both time limits.

route and road travel, the walker reached Open Plot 4-1 in drawn,
