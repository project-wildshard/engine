# DF8: the dock's last long frames on the iPhone 13 (mini-driftwood13m)

- `debug.taskStamp` (`core/taskStamp.ts`, off by default): the first page script wraps timers, animation frames,
  microtasks, listeners and `on*` handlers, the DOM observers and promise reactions, and stamps every callback of 2 ms or
  more; a 10 ms probe timer says how late it ran. `route-bench --frames` splits an "other" long frame into a stamped task,
  main-thread work nothing wraps (`held`), or a free main thread (`free`), because WebKit's Timeline gives zero timestamps
  on the phone (iOS 18.6.2).
- The frame log keeps update splits over 18 ms with parts of 1 ms, so the 21 to 35 ms one-off stalls name their lap.
- The first stamped warm boot named one dock stall: about 20 s after control the road backing hands the whole road
  meshes over to their tile pools in one step (`grid.road tiles` 32 ms, a 32 ms render, then about 170 ms with the main
  thread held and no callback running: one 245 ms frame). `debug.roadHandoffPaced` (off until phone pairs decide) hands
  one material over a step. The probe also renews a WeakRef sentinel, so a held frame says whether a collection ran in it
  (the Heap agent's collection times are all one value on this phone). Promise reactions are stamped only with the slot
  at `"all"`: their wrappers allocate a closure a call.
