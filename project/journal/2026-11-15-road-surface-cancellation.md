# Bound optional road surface preparation

> Written by: Codex

PZ24e1 bounds the optional native road maps at five seconds. The
admitted owner aborts both isolated reads on timeout or session exit;
the entry can take the unchanged procedural path immediately, even if
an injected reader or upload never settles. Late textures are disposed
without publication, and the residency claim is surrendered.

KTX2 callers may supply an AbortSignal. Such a read never joins the
shared fetch/transcode wave, so cancelling one session cannot cancel
another caller. The loader also refuses a cancelled decode result.
Focused tests cover timeout, owner exit, late disposal and isolation
from a simultaneous ordinary read.
