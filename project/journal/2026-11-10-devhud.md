# Developer HUD on phones (A14)

Jake, on his iPhone (402x654) on staging, Driftwood, Developer mode on:
"the screen is chaotic, right? Let's have someone fix this." His layout,
relayed at 12:02 Cancun: MEM a small card next to the FPS chip, the net
debug text inside the FPS box, the quest text down and right, the chat
bar bottom left above MOVE, and a script for the worst-case HUD. QA row
15 then: hide the chat and the mic while a menu is open.

- The quest chip under PAUSE was not a Developer bug. quest.css opened
  with a comment holding the glob `src/game/*/quest/*`; its `*/` closed
  the comment early and the stray word `quest` became the first rule's
  selector (`quest .ws-quest-obj`). The chip lost `position: absolute`
  and fell unstyled into the top-left corner on every page, phone and
  desktop. Fixed the comment; check-css now refuses a selector that
  names an element no page has.
- The memory strip sat at top + 150 px full width; the net readout was
  a `pre` in the status column (`band.3`) with the row glass behind one
  line, stepped down under the strip by measuring text. Both were
  drawn over the world. New `ui/perfDock.ts`: a row on PAUSE's line
  that holds the fps pill and small cards (the memory card "mem 1.03"),
  and sections that open with the frame meter's panel (the net block,
  the G168 alert, the hidden-level label, the memory-limit warning, the
  chunk boundary warning). A section that wants attention puts a red
  "!" on the pill. Desktop shows the alert sections at the top left
  (its line is click-through, so there is no panel to open).
- Landscape touch screens now get the pill and its panel too. A phone
  held sideways shows the rotate gate anyway (portrait only).
- Chat: a stylesheet instead of inline styles. On touch it sits over
  MOVE, above the SPRINT tag and the first hint; a disc in MOVE's corner
  of the thumb row lifts it; the USE band yields MOVE's column to it
  online; it never grows into the HOVER / SWAP column (oldest lines clip
  first). `<html data-ws-menu>` (layers.ts) hides it under any menu or
  panel; voice stays connected. FirstHints treats the chat row as an
  obstacle.
- `scripts/hud-worst.mjs`: the worst-case HUD, every element on at
  once, per browser x size x Developer mode, with the layout check
  (`--check`, `--menu`). `test/hud-layout-browser.test.ts` runs it by
  name in the browser lane.

Commits: the quest chip fix and the check-css guard first; then the dock
(perfDock.ts and its users); then the chat row and the menu mirror;
then the worst-case script and its browser test.
