# 2026-11-11: a lever-action from rows (TE-ENG2)

> Written by: Claude (opus-teeng2)

TE-ENG2 (meta `docs/plans/TEMPLATE-E2E-PLAN.md`) unblocks BM-3: Brasscap's rifle could not take a lever-only subset of
Pine's native assembly, so a declared `kit.lever` weapon row now does the job for any data shard. Opt-in and additive:
Pine's native rifle and every shard without a lever row are unchanged.

- Rows: a weapon row with `family: "kit.lever"` and a `firearm` profile (`action: "lever"`, `reloadMode: "perRound"`, a
  `lever` block), a `weapon.ranged`-keyed context, and `targets.itemActions` naming it (3 takes it, 4 reloads it). Format
  e51eec6 lets an item scene name a kit.lever and need no script binding.
- Server (`declaredKitItems.ts`): each player's seat runs Pine's `RangedAction.Cycle` and a hitscan from the server's eye
  along the wire's aim at any body the host holds (`nativeRangedHit`), stopped by walls. It starts on its rack; the
  rack's scene or prompt (judged near the prompt) takes it into hand. Rounds, reserve, reloading and taken reach the
  holder as owned lines (`leverLines`); the seat saves with the player. Reload acts route to it (`cellWorld.ts`).
- Page (`weaponFamily.ts` `DeclaredLever`): the row's GLB (or a `kit.rifle` stand-in) in hand, hidden until taken; its own
  cycle for the feel (recoil, lever throw, flash at the `muzzle` empty); presses and R on the wire; the server's count at
  rest; its state fills the HUD's Rounds strip. `itemFamilies` gains `locked`, so a locked family is not unlocked at
  install.

Tests: `test/declared-lever.test.ts` (rows-only fixture on Template: taken only near the rack, six rounds that land on
the blob, a dry reload of five then a top-up of one, a save keeps it) and `test/declared-lever-view.test.ts` (the page's
half). Preview: Brasscap with a local lever row and BM-3's rifle GLB validates and plays solo in headless Chromium.
