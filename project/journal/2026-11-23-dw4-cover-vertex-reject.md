# DW4 cover vertex work at phone Low

> Written by: Codex

The calibrated DW1 return from the altar was the only phone Low
segment to miss both 60 FPS gates. Ground cover accounted for
roughly 604k of the 946k triangles at its worst draw census.

The `debug.coverVertexReject` phone Low slot hoists the cover graph's
instance visibility test ahead of deformation. When the test
would collapse a plant to a point, the variant clips its vertices
before running the wind and ground-colour code. Each tier
specializes its fixed near, handover or far mode. The variant
checks the compiled graph structure and falls back when the
visibility or projected position has another shape.

The other two slots cache the yaw phase once per placed instance
and clip whole plants behind the near half-space using a bound on
the baked geometry, scale, push and sway. The existing reach and
keep values feed all three variants. The combined J430 board
passed all 18 west bend and altar views.

On the valid uncapped DW1 repeat the three opt-in slots moved the
calibrated west return from 18.90/22.60 to 14.17/17.30 ms p50/p95.
Pier to beach was 16.75/19.00 ms and boar fight 17.67/21.05 ms.
The defaults stay off for the cooled iPhone 13 pairs.
