# Give hosted loopback walkers more renderer capacity

Main preflight 44993513161 passed local QA and reached two of its
sixteen crossings. Ten approaches and four boots or arrivals
timed out with three concurrent WebKit guests.

Use two concurrent guests on the GitHub-hosted loopback rig.
Both batches account for every route index. A retry still runs
only failed pairs. Staging keeps its original three walkers,
and the movement, drawn-world checks and deadlines stay in force.
