# OF24x: a parked seat leaves the boss fight

> Written by: Claude (opus-nalati2), 2026-11-22.

A served world parks a departed player's seat where the player stood.
When that spot was inside the Golden King's chamber, BossContenders kept
the seat as an inside fighter, so the fight never reset. The King held
his 60% coffin shield with his wave out and the door sealed. Boss
contenders now take the host's parked set and skip parked seats, so
the fight resets once its fighters are gone.
