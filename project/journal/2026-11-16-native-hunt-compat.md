# Native hunting compatibility during the M1 move

The daily partial M1 land must run existing frozen products. Pine's deer and
Nalati's sheepdog omit tuning in their older native source contracts. Removing
the historical defaults before those producers rebuild makes real worlds fail
while adopting bodies, before combat or travel can run.

The two older native managers explicitly opt into their existing v0 contract.
The hunting core requires complete numbers by default, and the layered-row
resolver still refuses missing or invalid authored tuning before installing a
body. Existing explicit native tuning is validated and never replaced.

Final removal of the native compatibility numbers remains open in ONE-FORMAT
OF5b, after normal producer rebuild, cook, publication and pinning. This partial
land does not complete M1 or claim that those numbers have left the engine.
