opus-chunk4, meta CHUNK-PARAM-REMOVAL CP16 (J522).

The parity CLI, its own modules, the scorecard, hover-touch and the playground harness all booted a zone alone by its
address. Nothing runs them now (the gpu-perf nightly last ran 2026-10-03 and isn't installed), so they retire; the
parity baselines stay, since the budgets read them as ceiling sources.

The worker-client harness isn't a one-shard tool: it proves death, partner, hits and the Driftwood walk on the client players
run. opus-nalati2's tap rewrite failed mostly because the grid page's `world.animals` and `world.interactables` are the
platform level's, empty; the entered cell's creatures and prompts live in its region. The grid's debug handle now hands
them out (`shard.grid.animals()`, `.interactables()`) and the scripts read those first. Death went 1 of 3 to 3 of 3,
partner 0 of 2 to 2 of 2, creature-hit 0 of 6 to 4 of 6. What still fails looks like the grid itself (hits present twice
with no number, a bear's blow comes without its blow feel or windup), so it's a QA row, and these checks are its proof.
recovery-menu retires: it opened the one-shard page's `glreload` address, and the grid's graphics recovery is a record.
